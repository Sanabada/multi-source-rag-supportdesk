# SupportDesk Admin Tips
Date: 2026-03-10

## Handling Refund Questions
Admins should check the first purchase date before approving a refund. The official policy allows refunds within 14 days of the first purchase.

## Reducing App Crashes
Most browser crashes are caused by old cache data or conflicting extensions. Ask users to clear cache, disable extensions, and retry in a supported browser.

## Managing Plan Limits
Free workspaces are best for trials because they support 3 agents and 100 tickets per month. Team workspaces support larger teams with 15 agents and 5,000 tickets per month.

## Mobile Support Checklist
For mobile sync problems, confirm the app version, network connection, and whether the user recently changed their password.
