# SupportDesk Product Blog
Date: 2026-04-20

## Mobile Sync Improvements
The April release improved mobile sync reliability. The app now retries failed ticket and comment sync requests automatically when the device reconnects.

## Updated File Uploads
Business plan upload limits increased from 50 MB to 100 MB per attachment. Free and Team plans still use the 25 MB attachment limit.

## Export Scheduling
Business plan admins can now schedule weekly ticket exports from Admin Settings. The export file is delivered as a CSV download link.

## Login Help
Many login issues are caused by expired reset links or too many failed attempts. Reset links expire after 20 minutes, and locked accounts unlock automatically after 15 minutes.
