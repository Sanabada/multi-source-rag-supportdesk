# SupportDesk Product Documentation

## Login Problems
SupportDesk users sign in with an email address and password. If a user cannot log in, they should first check that the email address is correct and then reset the password. Accounts are locked for 15 minutes after five failed login attempts.

## Password Reset
Users can reset their password from the login page by selecting Forgot password. The reset link is valid for 20 minutes. If the link expires, the user should request a new reset email.

## Billing and Plans
SupportDesk has Free, Team, and Business plans. The Free plan allows 3 agents and 100 tickets per month. The Team plan allows 15 agents and 5,000 tickets per month. The Business plan has unlimited tickets.

## Refund Policy
SupportDesk refunds paid subscriptions within 14 days of the first purchase. Monthly renewals are not refunded after the renewal date. Refund requests must be submitted from Billing Settings.

## Mobile Sync
The mobile app syncs tickets, comments, and notifications when the device is online. If sync stops, users should update the app, check network access, and sign out and sign back in.

## File Uploads
Agents can attach files to tickets. The maximum file size is 25 MB per attachment on Free and Team plans. Business plan workspaces can upload attachments up to 100 MB.

## App Crash Troubleshooting
If the web app crashes or freezes, users should refresh the page, clear browser cache, and disable browser extensions. If the issue continues, they should send the browser name, operating system, and screenshot to support.

## Data Export
Admins can export tickets as CSV from Admin Settings. Free and Team plans can export once per day. Business plan workspaces can schedule weekly exports.
